Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

GetOfYourBApp is a wellness nudge app that reminds desk workers to take short breaks. We take your privacy seriously.

Data we collect

GetOfYourBApp does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted anywhere.

Advertising

The free version of the app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for more information about how AdMob handles data.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications — to send wellness nudges
  • Run in background / ignore battery optimisation — to ensure nudges arrive reliably
  • Internet — required for displaying ads

Third party services

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle data.

Children

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
